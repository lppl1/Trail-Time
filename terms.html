Terms of Use

Trail & Time is a student project created for IS117 at NJIT. The trail guides, history and geography information on this site are for general information only.

  • Hike at your own risk. Trail conditions, distances and times can change. Always check with the park or trail authority before you go.
  • Photos. All photos on this site were taken by the site author on personal hiking trips and may not be reused without permission.
  • Shared content. If you send us photos or trail stories, you confirm they are your own and that we may feature them on this site.

Privacy Statement

We only collect the information you choose to send us through the email sign-up or the contact form, such as your name, email address and trail stories.

  • Your email address is only used to send Trail & Time updates.
  • We never sell or share your information with anyone else.
  • You can ask to be removed from updates at any time through theContact page.
